This proves one bounded external-client proposal and verified FHIR save on
loopback. It does not establish Muse, Grok Bot, eve, MCP Tasks, A2A, hosted
Medplum, clinical correctness, production authorization, or HIPAA readiness.

Task records a request; no patient contact or clinical follow-up was performed.
External model mode sends the request and synthetic chart projection to its
provider. **PHI is not permitted.**
